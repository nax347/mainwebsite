How It Works

From your first message to a targeted campaign. Here is how we evaluate, coordinate, and execute your music promotion.

1. Initial Contact & Submission

You reach out to us, sending your EPK (if available), website, social media links, or audio material for review.

2. Editorial Review & Offer

We manually review your material. If it fits, you receive a tailored promotion offer; if not, you get constructive feedback with tips so your next application hits the mark.

3. Material List & Deadline

Once we agree on a package, you receive our detailed material checklist. As soon as all required assets arrive completely and on time, we start.

4. Production & PR Execution

We roll out your campaign: EPK creation, video content snipping, and strategic media pitching while you focus on your music.
