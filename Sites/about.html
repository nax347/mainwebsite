About Us

Independent music needs more than algorithmic noise. It needs a voice, a strategy, and professional execution.

The truecatspr Mission

Founded as truecatspr, our agency bridges the gap for independent artists and labels. In an era where digital distribution is globally accessible, standing out requires more than just uploading tracks. We combine classical editorial PR, compelling press texts, and targeted playlist pitching with modern digital content-recycling to build authentic, long-term audience traction.

Markus Tobias Buchwalder

Managing Director & Operations

As the primary operational director, Markus steers day-to-day campaign work, music promotion, content snipping, online marketing, and direct artist guidance. His deep focus lies on shaping creative assets and positioning independent talent effectively in the digital landscape.

Holger Gechter

Co-Founder & Administration / Legal

Bringing over 20 years of robust entrepreneurial experience in sales and business structure, Holger oversees the administrative, legal, and contractual frameworks (including service agreements and transparent terms) to guarantee absolute reliability and safety for all partners.

The Two-Layer Principle

Our strength lies in a seamless division of labor: creative PR execution meets seasoned commercial oversight. This ensures that every campaign is not only culturally and musically sharp, but also legally and structurally sound from day one.
